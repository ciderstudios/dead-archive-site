Privacy Policy

Dead Archive is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no servers and no advertising, and nothing you type or tap leaves your device.

Dead Archive collects no personal data.

The game keeps your saved games and settings on your own device and nowhere else. Nothing is sent, nothing is sold, and there is no analytics or advertising code in the app.

01 · What the game stores

On your device, and only there

All of it lives in the app's private storage, managed by your device's operating system. Like the rest of your apps' data, it goes into your own device backups. Deleting Dead Archive deletes all of it.

Saved games
The game in progress, saved after every move, and the games you save yourself: where you are in the house, what you carry and what has happened so far. So you can close the game and carry on later.
Settings
Language, original text, monitor colour, scan lines and drawing speed. So you set them once.
Device language
The first time the game opens, it reads the language your device is set to, to start in English, Spanish or Portuguese. It keeps only the language it picked, which you can change in Settings. So the game opens in your language.

02 · What the game does not do

The short list

  • Accounts or sign-in
  • Network requests
  • Advertising
  • In-app purchases
  • Analytics
  • Tracking across apps
  • Location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone
  • Sale of data

Dead Archive has no advertising identifier and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to track you with.

03 · Children

Not made for them, and nothing collected from anyone

Dead Archive is a murder mystery, rated 13+ in most countries. It is not directed at children and is not in the App Store's Kids category. It collects no data from anyone, so it collects none from children either.

04 · Apple

What the App Store does on its own

When you buy the game, Apple handles the payment and may collect its own data under Apple's privacy policy, not ours. If you have chosen to share analytics and crash reports with developers in your device settings, Apple may show us aggregated, anonymous crash and usage figures in App Store Connect. They do not identify you, and only you can turn that sharing on or off, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

05 · Your rights

Nothing is held about you

Under the GDPR, the CCPA, Brazil's LGPD and similar laws, you can ask a company to show, correct, export or delete the personal data it holds about you. The publisher of Dead Archive holds none. To remove the game's data from your device, delete the app.

If you think anything on this page is wrong, tell us through the support page and we will look into it and correct it.

06 · Changes

Announced before, never after

If a later version adds anything that sends data off your device, this page will change before that version comes out, the date below will change with it, and the release notes will say so.

07 · Contact

Who publishes the game

Dead Archive is published by Cider Studios (CIDER STUDIOS LTDA, Itu, São Paulo, Brazil). Privacy questions, corrections and complaints go through the support page.
