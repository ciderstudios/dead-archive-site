Support

Stuck, found a bug, or the game did not understand you? Here is how to get help. Every message is read.

Report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/ciderstudios/dead-archive-site/issues. A GitHub account is free, and no purchase details are needed. You can write in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

01 · Before you write

Three things that make a bug fixable

  • Device and system version. Settings → General → About on your device.
  • Game version. At the bottom of the first screen, the case archive: CIDER STUDIOS · v1.0.0.
  • What happened. The room you were in, what you typed or tapped, and what the game answered.

A screenshot is worth all three.

02 · Common questions

Answered before you ask

I am stuck
Tap HINT. Every moment of the case has three hints, from a nudge to the answer. They are free, and reading one never costs a move. The solution is not on this page, so nobody reads it by accident.
I died
Tap STEP BACK on the screen that follows, and the last move is taken back. It is also in MENU. Death comes without warning, as it did in 1980; going back is the part that is new.
It does not understand me
Commands are two words, a verb and an object: OPEN DOOR, GET NOTE. If a word does not work, try another one. The buttons only offer words the game knows. The parser is the 1980 one, now also in Spanish and Portuguese. If a word you think it should know does not work, tell us: we add words.
The notes are in English
The notes in the house are the original 1980 drawings, so their writing is in English. When you read one, its text appears translated below the picture.
Lost progress
Saved games live only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, and there is no cloud save in this version. A backup of your device normally keeps them.
Refunds
Apple handles every purchase. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. The publisher cannot issue App Store refunds.
Privacy
The game collects nothing. The privacy policy has the details.

03 · About Case 1

Where the house comes from

Case 1, The Victorian House, is a remake of a 1980 game by Roberta and Ken Williams, released into the public domain in 1987. Its text, map, puzzles and drawings come from the original disk. Dead Archive is an independent remake by Cider Studios, not affiliated with or endorsed by the original authors.
